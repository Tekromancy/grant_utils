# Cooperative Capitalization & Revolving Debt Pool

**Awarding Agency:** U.S. Economic Development Administration (EDA)  
**Program:** Build to Scale (B2S) Venture Challenge  
**Funding Request:** $300,000 (3-Year Project Period)  
**Non-Federal Match:** 50% ($150,000 via local CDFI / credit union co-investments)  

---

## 1. Project Abstract
This project capitalizes an evergreen, non-extractive revolving debt pool providing patient growth capital ($15k–$75k) to emergent worker-owned cooperatives and social enterprises. 

## 2. Scope of Work & Milestones
- **Q1 2027:** Underwriting standards development and capitalization pipeline launch.
- **Q2 2027:** First round loan disbursements and technical assistance cohort kickoff.
- **Q3–Q4 2027:** Quarterly portfolio monitoring, job retention tracking, and reporting.

## 3. Projected Economic Impact
- 5 new worker-owned businesses financed.
- 45 living-wage jobs created or preserved.
- 100% principal repayment recycled into the community evergreen fund.
